# Lab Math 02 — Sets and Jaccard

The following English tasks are reconstructed from the teacher notebook's task headings and actual code, rather than claimed to be direct Word translations. Dataset and numeric values remain in the source demo and in the source-linked runnable solution.

## Task 1

Analyze Student Interests: find all unique subjects, students interested in Python AND AI, and those interested in Python OR Math.

## Task 2

Compare Two Students: read two names, handle invalid or duplicate names, and report intersection, union, and the difference of subject sets.

## Task 3

Calculate Jaccard Similarity: shared subject count divided by union subject count; guard empty union.

## Task 4

Find the Most Similar Pair: score each unordered pair of distinct students exactly once.

## Task 5

Recommend Study Partners: use threshold 0.5 and exclude self-recommendations.

## Task 6

Build a Simple Student Graph: undirected adjacency list with an edge at Jaccard >= 0.5 and isolated students included.
